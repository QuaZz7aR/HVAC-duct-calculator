# 0031. Side panel for adding fittings

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-23
- Source: owner idea, variant 3 of three mockups chosen by the owner; owner feedback on 2026-10-23 for every detail below; PR #28, #29, #30

## Context
Owner idea: the central list shows a fitting as a title only, new fittings are added from a panel that is always in view.

## Decision
- **Desktop** (`lg` and up): a sticky rail (15 rem, groups in two columns, larger type and glyphs for readability, owner 2026-10-23) on the left (`KindRail`) with the seven kind groups (`kind-groups.ts`: straight, elbow, reducer, cap, tap, tee, cross; glyph from `sketchSpecs`, `KindGlyph`). A group opens a list of its kinds beside the rail (designation, name, "+" or the count already in the list); picking one adds the row and closes the list. Escape or a press outside closes it.
- **Phone**: no rail; (since 2026-10-23 a «+» button at the right edge, see below; earlier a sticky row of quick buttons) (`quickKinds`: КВ, ПВ, КО, ПО, КП, ПП, КТ, ПТ) and «Усі типи…», which opens a bottom sheet with search by designation or name (`searchKinds`).
- A new row is inserted after the last row of its kind (`insertIndex`), so it lands in its kind section, gets the auto defaults, and the list scrolls to it and rings it briefly (`flash-card`, off under reduced motion).
- The card header is the title `N. КВ — Назва`; the kind `<select>` is gone. Changing the kind is still possible: the «⋯» button of the card opens «Змінити тип на…» with all kinds (same `setKind`, sizes restart from the new kind's defaults). The bottom «Додати деталь» button is gone (it added a row of the default kind).
- Fitting names and designations are unchanged. View state only, nothing persisted.
- **Discoverability**: the rail starts with a «+ Додати деталь» heading and the list heading has a hint line («оберіть її тип у панелі зліва»; on a phone it points at the buttons above the list).
- **View controls**: «Показати / Сховати всі схеми» and «Згорнути / Розгорнути всі деталі» in a separate «Вигляд» block under the kinds block (the button names the action it will do, with an eye / chevrons icon) live at the bottom of the rail so they stay in view; on a phone they stay above the list. The card view state moved from `FittingList` to `useCardView`, used by `Calculator` for both.
- **Default values**: a new row (and a row whose kind changes) starts with the default sizes of its kind (`form/defaults.ts`, the former beta sample sizes; placeholders, not recommendations) and is marked `preset`; the card shows «Типові значення за замовчуванням. Змініть їх під свій розмір.» until any field or the quantity is edited. The beta start state uses the same rows.
- **«Нагору»**: a third block at the bottom of the rail scrolls the page to the top (smooth, instant under reduced motion).
- **Phone button column**: a column of small round buttons at the right edge (`PhoneActions`, within reach of a thumb, not at the top of the screen; the column sits 15 % of the screen height above the bottom edge, lowered from 25 % at the owner's request 2026-10-23; the buttons are 48 px) holds the «show/hide all sketches» and «fold/unfold all» toggles and, after 300 px of scrolling, «to top».
- **Phone adding**: adding is done from the same column: a «+» button at the bottom opens a fan of round buttons along two arcs with an even angular step, both ending on the horizontal and vertical rays (quick kinds КВ, ПВ, КО… on the inner arc first, the outer arc ends with «⋯»; slots in `fanSlots`, at least about 60 px apart centre to centre, so a thumb does not hit a neighbour) and the «⋯» button that opens the bottom sheet with every kind and a search. The sticky header with quick buttons is gone, and the quick kinds are `quickKinds`.
- **Phone bottom sheet**: the search field is at the bottom of the sheet, within thumb reach, and is not focused on open so the keyboard stays down. The page behind does not scroll while the sheet is open: body overflow hidden plus a touchmove guard on the sheet (iOS ignores overflow hidden and chains touches from a list that does not scroll or is at its end); only the list pans, and the page is NOT pinned with position fixed, which broke the keyboard fit on iPhone. Results sit right above the search field (a flex spacer above them, not justify-end, so a long list still scrolls up to its first item) and while the keyboard is up the sheet is fitted to the visual viewport (top and bottom, iOS pans it over the page), so the list can always be scrolled to its first item.
- **Change-kind menu on desktop**: no scrolling; the kinds flow into 2 (tablet) or 3 (wide screen) columns by group, each group with its heading (повітроводи … хрестовини). On a phone it stays one list with one designation column and scrolls (a two-column phone variant was tried and rejected as too much).
- **Change-kind menu «⋯» position**: it hangs from the right edge of its button and is shifted back into the screen when it would leave it (`KindMenu`, measured on open), because on a phone the button is near the left edge.
- **Card buttons**: the row of «Згорнути», «Клонувати», «⋯», «Видалити» wraps onto a second line when it does not fit, e.g. in a card inside a repeated-kind section on a phone, instead of leaving the card.
- **Long field labels on a phone**: a field whose label with its note or action is longer than 17 characters (`isLongLabel`) takes both columns of the card (`wide` in `TextField`), so the two inputs of a row are never at different heights.
- **Fold all**: «Згорнути всі деталі» also scrolls to the top of the fittings list (`#items`, `scrollToList`), so the page does not end up below the shortened list. The fittings grid is `grid-cols-1` (minmax(0,1fr)) on a phone: without it the implicit column let cards overflow by 0.5 px and the page scrolled sideways.

## Consequences
- Changes [0030](0030-beta-start-state-and-card-view.md): the kind select and the bottom «add» button are gone, the view state lives in `useCardView`.
